Detect, compare, explain, fix, re-test

One loop that repeats after every deploy, so a fix that quietly breaks later does not go unnoticed.

  1. DetectFixed checks read your headers, cookies, redirects, CORS and TLS.
  2. CompareEach finding is marked new, unchanged, resolved or recurring.
  3. ExplainAI describes the impact from the scanner's own evidence.
  4. FixCopy ready code for Nginx, Apache, Django, Express or Netlify and Vercel.
  5. Re-testScan again and watch the finding move to Resolved.
